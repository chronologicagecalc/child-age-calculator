How to calculate a child’s age

Write today’s date above the date of birth and subtract each column from right to left – days, then months, then years.

  1. Days: if the birth day is bigger, borrow one month and add the number of days in the month before today’s month (28, 29, 30 or 31).
  2. Months: if the birth month is bigger, borrow one year and add 12 months.
  3. Years: subtract what is left. Write the answer as years;months;days.

Example – birthday 20 June 2019, age on 5 November 2026

Growing up

Child age stages at a glance

The names below are common everyday labels. Every child develops at their own pace, so use them as a rough guide only.

Newborn0–4 weeks

The first month – ages are usually given in days and weeks.

Infant1–12 months

Ages are counted in months, from first smiles to first steps.

Toddler1–3 years

Many parents still say “18 months” or “30 months” at this stage.

Preschooler3–5 years

Play, language and early learning before formal school.

School-age5–11 years

Primary or elementary school years.

Preteen11–13 years

The move towards secondary or middle school.

Teenager13–17 years

Secondary or high school until adulthood at 18.

Babies & toddlers

Why little ones are counted in months

A baby at 7 months and a baby at 11 months are both “under one”, yet they are at very different stages. That is why health checks, clothing sizes and baby books use months – and why the calculator always shows the total months too.

Most families switch to years at around two, though “30 months” is still common on medical forms.

Born early?

Corrected age for premature babies

A baby born before 37 weeks has two ages: the chronological age from their birthday, and a corrected (adjusted) age from their original due date. Health professionals usually look at corrected age when checking growth and development for the first couple of years.

FAQ

Child age questions

How do I calculate my child’s exact age?

Subtract the date of birth from today’s date in three columns – days, months and years. If the days are negative, borrow one month and add the number of days in the month before today’s month. If the months are negative, borrow one year and add 12 months.

What does an age like 4;7;12 mean?

It is a short way of writing 4 years, 7 months and 12 days. Teachers, therapists and psychologists often write a child’s age this way on reports and test forms.

Until what age should I use corrected age?

For babies born prematurely, corrected age is commonly used to judge growth and development until around two years old. Your child’s doctor or health visitor can tell you what applies to your baby.

Why do babies’ ages get counted in months?

Babies change so quickly that months – and in the first weeks, days and weeks – give a much clearer picture than years. Many parents switch to years after about two years.

How is a February 29 birthday handled?

The calculator uses real calendar months, so a child born on February 29 is counted correctly in leap and non-leap years. In non-leap years, families usually celebrate on February 28 or March 1.

Is my child’s date of birth stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser and nothing you enter is sent or saved.
